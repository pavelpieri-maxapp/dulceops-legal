Privacy Policy — DulceOps

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Version: 1.2

This policy explains what happens to information in the DulceOps app ("the App"), made by Pavel Pieri, doing business as PieriStudiosCo, Texas, United States ("we", "us"). It applies to every country where the App is available.

1. The short version

  • DulceOps works offline and on your device only. What you enter is stored on your phone in an encrypted database.
  • We do not collect your business data, your customers' data or how you use the App. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies.
  • We cannot see, sell or share your data, because it never reaches us.
  • Purchases of DulceOps Pro are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  • If you email us, we receive what you choose to send.

2. Information stored on your device

You decide what to enter. It can include:

  • your business profile: name, logo, owner, phone, email, address, website, tax or license number, currency, prices and settings;
  • customers: names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, allergies and notes;
  • orders, estimates, accepted versions, drawn signatures, notes and screenshots of acceptances, payments, receipts, deliveries, delivery photos and signatures, reference photos of orders, receipt photos of expenses, and the customizations of each order (how they are made and whether they were asked for, offered, accepted or declined);
  • recipes, supply prices and the currency each is bought in, your exchange rate, allergens, food-label details, inventory lots with expiry dates, your catalog of customizations with their costs and prices, products, expenses, fixed costs, employees, work days and pay;
  • your Pro status, trial use, tester-code status, and the install ID and activation code if you use one.

This information stays in the App's private storage on your device, protected by the device and by database encryption (SQLCipher). Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for the App, so this information is not copied to Google's servers by the system.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal data through the App. The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

The only time the App uses the internet is to talk to Google Play (or the Apple App Store) about DulceOps Pro: to show prices, process a purchase and check what you bought. That exchange is between your device and the Store.

4. Purchases

When you buy DulceOps Pro, the Store processes the payment under its own privacy policy:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

The App receives only the purchase status (which plan, whether it is active or pending) and keeps it on your device. We may see sales reports from the Store, as every developer does, which do not identify your business data.

5. Sharing documents and files

The App never shares anything on its own. When you tap Share, it creates the file (PDF, CSV, calendar event or backup) in a temporary private folder and hands it to the app you choose (WhatsApp, email, Drive, printer…). From then on, that app's privacy policy applies. Temporary files are replaced the next time you share.

6. Device features the App uses

  • Internet: only for the Store, as described above.
  • Photos and files: only when you choose an image (logo, product or recipe photo, delivery photo, expense receipt, order reference photo, acceptance screenshot) or a backup file, through your phone's own picker. The App does not read your gallery or files otherwise.
  • Calendar: "Add to calendar" opens your calendar app with the event filled in; you save it there. The App does not read or change your calendar and asks for no calendar permission.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders (More → Phone reminders). Your phone schedules them itself, with the names of the customers to contact; nothing is sent over the internet. You can turn them off in the App or in your phone's settings.
  • Saving and printing: "Download" opens your phone's own "Save to…" screen, where you choose the place; "Print" opens Android's print screen. The App only gets access to the one file you save.
  • The App does not use your location, contacts, microphone, or advertising ID.

7. Your customers' and employees' information

When you store information about your customers or employees, you decide why and how it is used, so under many privacy laws you are the "controller" of that information (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, Brazil's LGPD, or similar laws). Because that information stays on your device and we never receive it, we are not your processor.

You are responsible for:

  • having a valid reason to keep it and telling those people as your law requires;
  • keeping it secure and only as long as needed;
  • answering their requests to see, correct or delete their information. You can edit or delete customers, orders and other records in the App.

8. Backups

Backups are files you create. They are encrypted (AES-256) with a password only you know, and you decide where to keep them. We never receive your backups, cannot open them and cannot recover a forgotten password. Your trial, tester access and purchase status are not included in backups.

9. Emails to support

If you write to pieristudiosco.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and what you send, and use it only to answer you and to improve the App. We keep these messages as long as needed for that purpose and then delete them. Our email is provided by Google, which may process it in the United States or other countries.

Please do not send your customers' personal data to support. If a backup or screenshot is needed to solve a problem, we will tell you how to remove personal data first.

10. Children

The App is a business tool for adults (18+). It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, the Philippines' Data Privacy Act, Vietnam's personal-data law, China's PIPL or similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data-protection authority.

Because the App's data stays on your device, you exercise most of these rights directly in the App (edit, delete, back up, uninstall). For anything we hold (support emails), write to us and we will answer within the time your law requires. For Store purchases, contact Google or Apple.

We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.

12. Security

We designed the App to keep data on your device, encrypted, and to send nothing to us. No system is perfectly secure: protect your device with a screen lock, keep it updated, and keep your backup password safe.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this policy, change its date and, for important changes, tell you in the App before they apply.
